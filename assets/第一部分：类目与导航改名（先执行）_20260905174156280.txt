• How to Choose 分步选型：
1. 先定功能：装液管看水分/流动 → Moisture Indicator（SGN 系列）；装压缩机曲轴箱看油位/储液器看液位 → Oil Level（SGR 系列），两者不可混用。
2. 再定连接：铜管永久焊接选 Brazed ODF；需现场拆卸维护选 SAE Flare（喇叭口纳子）；管路需一头直连另一头内螺纹选 SAE Flare M/F；钢管/容器锥螺纹口选 NPT。
3. 定尺寸：按液管外径/连接口尺寸选 1/4"–1-1/8" 对应型号。
4. 制冷剂：HCFC/HFC 均适用；颜色指示：绿=干燥、黄=潮湿。

视液镜类目选型 FAQ（第 4 条）
Q: Which sight glass do I need — a moisture indicator for the liquid line or an oil level sight glass?
A: Moisture indicator sight glasses (SGN series, brazed ODF or SAE flare) mount on the refrigerant liquid line and show moisture content (green = dry, yellow = wet) and refrigerant flow. Oil level sight glasses (SGR series) mount on the compressor crankcase or liquid receiver and show oil/liquid level only — they have no moisture indicator. Choose brazed ODF for permanent soldered installation, SAE flare for removable service connections, or M/F for direct tube-to-tube flare joints.
