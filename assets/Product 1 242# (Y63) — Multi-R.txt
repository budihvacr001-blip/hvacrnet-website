FAQ

Which refrigerants can this gauge read?
The 242# (Y63) is a multi-refrigerant gauge with a printed pressure–temperature scale for R22, R134a, R404A and R407C, so one model covers most conventional HCFC/HFC systems.

Why is the gauge filled with oil?
The internal damping oil absorbs compressor vibration and stops the pointer from jumping or fluttering, giving a stable, easy-to-read value on running equipment and extending gauge life.

How does it mount?
It is an axial (back-entry) gauge with a flanged edge and three mounting holes, designed to be bolted flush into an equipment or control panel.

What is its lower temperature limit?
This standard model is scaled from −5 °C to +120 °C. For low-temperature freezer service down to −40 °C, select the 191# model instead.
